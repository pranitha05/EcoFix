5-R E-Waste Disposal Guide

The 5-R Framework

  • Reduce: Buy durable hardware with modular components.
  • Repair: Replace damaged sub-assemblies before abandoning whole units.
  • Reuse: Repurpose outdated components for home automation or secondary tasks.
  • Resell: Sell functional scrap boards to local hobbyists or refurbishers.
  • Recycle: Drop unfixable logic boards at verified e-waste collection bins.
